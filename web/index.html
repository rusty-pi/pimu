pimu in the browser

A Raspberry Pi 4 (BCM2711) booting its real firmware on the VideoCore VPU and then Linux, all emulated in WebAssembly in this tab.

idle 


Downloads about 13 MB of firmware and a kernel from raspberrypi/firmware on GitHub,
and needs about 1 GiB of memory. The emulation is slow: expect minutes to the end of the kernel's boot.
There is no root filesystem, so the boot ends in a kernel panic, as the command-line example does.
